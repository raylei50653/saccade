## Why superseded

The coverage framing asked:

```text
which grammar × validation cells are supported?
```

The stronger framing asks:

```text
how is an observed safe/productive region packaged as a stable,
comparable, transferable, and action-bounded research asset?
```

Grammar remains a candidate-region producer. Coverage is now a subordinate decision map used to decide whether a new producer may add asset value:

```text
new unique productive masks
more full-neighborhood thickness
more multi-sequence support
higher productive capacity
better transfer retention
clearer intervention semantics
```

No grammar extension should be justified merely because a coverage-table cell is empty.

## Retained C0 baseline
