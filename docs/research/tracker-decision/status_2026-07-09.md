**Decision: keep both as production default.**

| Stage | Control | Production |
|:--|:--|:--|
| Cost Π reward | `stability_cost_w` YAML | **0.20** |
| Auction bid bias | `SACCADE_STABILITY_W` env | **0.1** if unset |

Evidence (2026-07-09, SDP, double-buffer, 7-seq):

| Path | Result |
|:--|:--|
| **s** (primary) | A (both) best: IDF1 **78.4**, AssA **70.0**, IDs **425**; both stages earn their keep (~0.9–1.0 IDF1 each) |
| **m** (capacity) | A best IDF1 **80.3**; C competitive (80.1); **bid required**; **cost additivity weak / near-noise** (A−C ≈ 0.2 IDF1) |

```text
✓ keep both on headline s/m
✗ no dual-stability behavior PR
✗ do not flip SACCADE_STABILITY_W or stability_cost_w from this workstream
✗ no jitter unless a small-delta decision actually needs it
```

Nuance to preserve in write-ups: **m cost additivity is weak/near-noise**; that does **not** overturn global keep-both driven by **s**.
